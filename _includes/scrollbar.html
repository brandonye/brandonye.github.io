{%- comment -%} The floating scrollbar: a thin bar over the right edge of the page, shown while you scroll (or point at it) and fading out a moment after. It can be dragged. Only on computers whose own scrollbars take up room: head.html spots those and adds .float-scroll, and site.css hides the browser's own scrollbar there. Styles are under "Floating scrollbar" in site.css. {%- endcomment -%}
